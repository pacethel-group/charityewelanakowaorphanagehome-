THE WOMAN BEHIND THE MISSION

Hajiya Bilkisu Musa

Former heavyweight champion. Caregiver. Advocate for education. Builder of a home where vulnerable children are treated as family.

Hajiya Bilkisu MusaFormer Heavyweight Champion

A life of strength expressed not only in competition, but in compassion, responsibility and service.

A HEART FOR EVERY CHILD

She treats every orphan as her own child.

Hajiya Bilkisu Musa is the heart behind a vision of care that refuses to measure a child's worth by circumstance. She considers every one of the children in her care as her own biological child and seeks to give them the protection, discipline, love and opportunity they deserve.

Although she does not have biological children of her own, she has embraced the responsibility of ensuring that children under her care are not left behind. For her, education is not optional: every child under her care should go to school.

Stand With Her Mission
A BROADER VISION OF EMPOWERMENT

More Than Shelter

The vision reaches beyond providing a place to sleep. It is about helping children and families develop the confidence, education and practical capacity to build sustainable futures.

Education First

Schooling is treated as a non-negotiable foundation. Children should have access to learning, educational materials and the support they need to remain engaged.

Vocational Training

Practical skills can create pathways to independence. Vocational training gives young people an opportunity to discover abilities and develop useful trades.

Sports Training

Sport develops discipline, resilience, teamwork and confidence. The athletic spirit is also part of the culture of strength and perseverance.

Widow Empowerment

The mission extends to widows, helping create pathways toward dignity, resilience and greater economic independence.
