Our story

More than a home.
A place to belong.

A home for care, learning and belonging

A child needs more than a roof. Every child deserves to be safe, encouraged, educated and treated with dignity.

Charity Ewela Nakowa Orphanage Home is based at No. 70 UTAN GRA, Jos, Plateau State. The home supports children with daily care and opportunities to learn, develop and build confidence.

Our work is grounded in a simple belief: when a community makes room for its children, hope has somewhere to grow.

THE HEART OF THE HOMEHajiya Bilkisu Musa

Biography · Leadership

One caring heart
can change many lives.

Hajiya Bilkisu Musa is the founder and caregiver associated with the home’s mission to offer children a family-centred place of support.

Her story reflects the conviction that children should be met with kindness, encouraged in their education and given the space to grow with dignity. The home carries that commitment forward through everyday care and community support.

“We do not have much, but we have enough to share — and sharing makes it warm.”— A message of care from the home
