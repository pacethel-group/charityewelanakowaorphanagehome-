OUR STORY

A Palace of Hope

Every child deserves safety, dignity, opportunity and love. Explore how Charity Ewela Nakowa Orphanage Home is working toward that vision.

Love, dignity and a future

Charity Ewela Nakowa Orphanage Home exists to give vulnerable children more than temporary shelter. The vision is a safe environment where children can grow in dignity, receive education, eat well, develop character and believe that their future matters.

Our Vision

A community where no vulnerable child is forgotten and every child has a genuine opportunity to thrive.

Our Values

Dignity, compassion, integrity, responsibility, education, faith and transparent stewardship.

Our Promise

To put the wellbeing of children at the centre of our work and invite supporters to participate with generosity and accountability.

CHARITY EWELA NAKOWA ORPHANAGE HOME

Building a safe, dignified and hopeful future for vulnerable children in Jos, Plateau State.
