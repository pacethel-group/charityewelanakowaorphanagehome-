Brief History of Founder

Hajiya Bilkisu Musa, Founder of Charity Ewela Nakowa Orphanage Home and Sports Development Club, No 70 UTAN GRA Jos, Plateau State. Former Nigerian weightlifting champion, mother to 40 children.

She began her sports career as a wrestler in Bauchi State in 1988. From wrestling, she was employed as a brown belt Judoka and Silver Medalist in Boxing in 1989 with Bauchi State Sports Council. Thereafter, she moved to Jos, Plateau State in 1990 with Plateau State Sports Council as a weightlifter, where she won 42 Gold medals for Plateau State, becoming an unbeatable lifter in the Nigerian Weightlifting Federation...

Her journey from Bauchi mats to world podiums mirrors the resilience she now instills in every child.

Training Era

In 1995 she became the first woman in Africa to win Silver Medal in the World Senior Weightlifting Championship held in Gongzhou China. She didn't stop there. In 1997 World Weightlifting Championship held in Chiang Mai Thailand she won Silver Medal for Nigeria. In 1999 World Weightlifting Championship held at Peace and Friendship Stadium in Piraeus Athens Greece she lifted a total of 252.5kg to place third best in the world. In 1999 7th All African Games held in Johannesburg South Africa she won three Gold Medals for Nigeria and set three African Records in Snatch, Jerk and Total event. In that same competition the Nigerian female weightlifting team won 21 Gold medals.
